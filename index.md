## Privacy Policy (EN)

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Controller: BE STUDIO, bugra545422@gmail.com

Short version: Old Racer has no accounts and we run no server that collects your personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

### What is stored on your device only

- Progress: tokens, free rounds, level and XP, best scores, unlocked tracks, settings and language, your player name, and any 3D model (STL) you upload. We never receive this data. Clearing the app data deletes it.

### What leaves your device, and to whom

- Online rooms (peer-to-peer, WebRTC): to connect, the Game contacts the public PeerJS signalling service and public STUN/TURN servers, which see your IP address and technical connection data. Players in your room can see your player name, vehicle and IP address. Join rooms only if you accept this.
- Internet radio: the Game requests station lists from the public radio-browser.info service and streams audio from the radio station you pick. Those services can see your IP address. Radio is optional.
- Purchases: tokens are bought through Google Play. Google processes payment data under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card details.
- Ads: this version contains no third-party advertising SDK. If we add one (for example Google AdMob), it may use your advertising ID, and we will update this policy and the store disclosures first.

### What we do not do

- We do not sell your data, build advertising profiles, or collect your contacts, location, photos or microphone data.

### Children

- The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

### Your rights

- Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access or delete on our side. Under KVKK (Türkiye), GDPR (EEA/UK) and similar laws you may contact us with any request; we will answer within the legal period. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

### Security and retention

- Local data stays on your device until you clear it. Third-party services keep technical logs under their own policies.

### Changes

- We will publish changes in the Game and on this page.
